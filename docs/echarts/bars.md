# Bars

`-Type bar` with one or more `-Y` columns or `-GroupBy` for one series per group. `-Stacked` stacks the
series, `-Normalize` stacks them to 100 %, `-Horizontal` turns the bars, `-DataLabels` writes the
values on them, `-NegativeColor` colours negative values. A bar chart can switch to lines or stacks
from its toolbox.
