# Shares

`pie`, `donut` and `funnel` take a name (`-X`) and a value (`-Y`); labels show value and share.
`-Rose` turns a pie into a Nightingale chart. `treemap` and `sunburst` take a hierarchy in `-Path`
(columns from the root, or one column "a/b/c") and zoom in on a click. Labels inside a filled shape are
dark or white, whichever contrasts more with the fill.
